Version Two

In version 2, I added a fire sensor and an M2 smoke sensor as well as the water pump. This allowed me to get multiple readings on what was happening. Adding the smoke sensor was needed because we could now use two different systems to predict and detect fires. On the other hand, if a fire sensor detects fire, there will be no second sensor confirmation. Fire prediction is also possible using a combination of sensors. Using the temperature sensor and smoke sensor, we can predict that a fire may occur in a certain area if we get two high readings on the temperature and smoke sensor. The water pump is activated when we have a high temperature and smoke warning or when we have a fire warning from the fire sensor. The water pump is giving electrical interference to the screen and corrupting data coming to the screen. Random characters are appearing on the screen.

Version Three

In version 3, I have solved the electrical interference problem. To solve the electrical interference problem, I added a 100 microfarad capacitor between the 5V and the ground pins of my LCD screen. I also added a 100 microfarad capacitor between the positive and negative wires of the water pump. I also put a diode between the positive and negative wires of the water pump. After doing all of this, I was still receiving some electrical interference mainly due to the magnetic field generated by the magnets spinning the motor. The only way to solve this (because I didn't have a chamber to block the magnetic field) was through moving the pump far away. I moved the pump two feet away from the rest of the circuit. After that I received little to no interference on the screen.
