01 / Background

Building to Understand.

My curiosity about flight began with a balsa wood glider. When it stalled and dropped into the grass, I started experimenting with its balance and launch angle rather than simply trying again. That instinct to investigate, test, and improve has shaped how I approach engineering. Today, I explore aerospace through model aircraft, experimental aerodynamics, computational analysis, and engineering projects aimed at solving real problems.

03 / Engineering Philosophy

Question. Build. Test. Improve.

I am most interested in the space between theory and reality: where a prediction meets an experiment, a design encounters its limitations, and unexpected results lead to better questions. I want to understand not just whether something works, but why it works and how it can be improved.
