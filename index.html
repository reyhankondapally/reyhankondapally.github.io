01 / About

Building to Understand.

My curiosity about flight began with a balsa wood glider. When it stalled and dropped into the grass, I started investigating its balance and launch angle rather than simply trying again. That instinct to question, test, and improve continues to shape how I approach engineering. Through independent projects, I explore how theoretical ideas translate into physical designs and real-world behavior.
