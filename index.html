01 / About

Building to Understand

Hi, I'm Reyhan.

I'm an aspiring aerospace engineer with a passion for understanding how things work and turning ideas into reality. My interest in aviation began with building model airplanes and experimenting with their flight, which eventually grew into projects involving aerodynamics, computational fluid dynamics (CFD), and wind tunnel testing.

I enjoy going beyond what I learn in the classroom by designing, building, testing, and improving my own projects. Whether I'm studying airflow over a wing, developing new aircraft systems, or bringing a CAD model to life, I'm always looking for ways to learn something new. To me, engineering is about more than making something work—it's about understanding why it works, learning from what doesn't, and using that knowledge to build something better.
