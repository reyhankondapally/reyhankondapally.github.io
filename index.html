Building to Understand.

03 / Engineering Philosophy

Question. Build. Test. Improve.

I am interested in the space between theory and reality: where predictions meet experiments, designs encounter limitations, and unexpected results lead to better questions. I want to understand not just whether something works, but why it works and how it can be improved.
